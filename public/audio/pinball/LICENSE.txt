Audio samples in this folder are individual notes from the FluidR3_GM soundfont
(instruments: electric_piano_1, string_ensemble_1, pad_2_warm, fretless_bass).

FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen, MIT license, via gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

The sequence (composition and arrangement) that plays these samples is original
and lives in src/apps/pinball/music.ts.
